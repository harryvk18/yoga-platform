# YogOm — Business Model (Pre-Launch · France)

> *"YogOm" is the working product name. No school, university, person, or real brand is referenced anywhere in this document or the product. Internal document, EUR.*

## 1. Positioning

**"Le yoga pour tous — tous les corps, tous les budgets."** *(Yoga for everyone — every body, every budget.)*

A mobile-first studio-yoga membership for France that takes yoga off its premium-boutique pedestal (often €18–25 a class, €90–150/month) and makes it an affordable, no-strings habit — anchored by a genuinely **free, no-card first in-person class**.

Two promises do the heavy lifting with a French audience:
1. **Accessible price** — the flagship Studio plan is **8 classes for €69,99/month (under €9 a class)**, versus €18–25 elsewhere.
2. **Sans engagement** — cancel anytime, pause anytime, no contract. In France this is a *major* trust lever: consumers are wary of gym lock-in, and the 2023 "résiliation en 3 clics" rules made effortless cancellation a public expectation. We make it our headline, not our fine print.

We position not against premium studios but against *"je m'y mettrai un jour"* — removing the three reasons people never start: **cost, commitment, intimidation.** The Indian roots of the practice are honoured in brand soul ("Né en Inde, pratiqué en France"), with a calm, French-minimal execution.

## 2. Target Segments

- **Urban beginners (25–45)** in Paris and large metros who assume studios are "too expensive / too advanced for me."
- **Budget-conscious actives & students** who want a routine but distrust gym contracts.
- **Adults 35–60** managing stress, back pain, sleep — who prefer guided in-person practice over an app alone.
- **Women** seeking welcoming, beginner-friendly classes near home or work.
- **Households / couples** — the Famille plan shares one affordable price.
- **Expats & international residents** in Paris/Lyon — served by the EN toggle.
- **Corporate wellbeing** (later channel) — via Gymlib / Swile / ClassPass-style aggregators and direct CE/CSE deals.

## 3. Pricing Tiers (EUR, TTC)

| Tier | Price | Interval | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Découverte** (Free) | €0 | forever | Curious first-timers — free in-person trial, no card |
| **En ligne** (Online · live from India) | €60 | / month | 8 live online sessions/month with a teacher in India |
| **Studio** (In-person — most popular) | **€69,99** | / month | The regular: 8 studio classes/month + roll-over |
| **Famille / Illimité** | €119 | / month | Households (up to 4) or unlimited solo practice |

**Découverte — €0**
- Free first in-person class at a partner studio, **no card**
- A taste of the app (booking, beginner info) — paid online classes not included free
- Upgrade anytime, never expires. *Nothing to cancel — no card on file.*

**En ligne — €60/month — live from India**
- **8 live online sessions/month**, taught in real time by a yoga teacher **in India** — authentic instruction from home (~€7.50/session)
- The differentiator: real Indian teachers, live — not a recorded library
- *Cancel/pause in 2 clicks, no fee; access to period end; 14-day withdrawal right (droit de rétractation).*

**Studio — €69,99/month** *(flagship)*
- **8 in-person studio classes/month** at any partner studio (under €9 a class)
- **Roll-over: up to 2 unused classes carry into the next month** — framed as "vos séances ne sont jamais perdues." Carried classes expire after that month (caps liability).
- Free mat/prop use · priority booking · add the live-from-India online sessions anytime
- *Cancel anytime no fee; pause up to 60 days/year (freezes billing); switch tiers anytime, prorated.*

**Famille / Illimité — €119/month**
- Unlimited in-person across partner studios; or up to **4 members** with individual profiles
- Includes the live-from-India online sessions for the household · monthly guest pass
- *Cancel/pause anytime; pause whole plan or per member.*

**Drop-in:** single classes from **€15** for occasional practice. Landing anchors the free trial, "moins de 9 € la séance," and the live-from-India online option.

## 4. Free-First-Class Funnel

The hook is a **genuinely free first in-person class, zero payment friction.**

1. **Waitlist capture** — name + mobile (+33) + email + GDPR consent. No card, ever, for the trial.
2. **Launch invite** — each signup gets an SMS/email (WhatsApp optional) with a unique trial code + a link to pick a partner studio, date, and beginner-friendly class nearby.
3. **The class** — walk in, show the code, free mat, 45–60 min welcome class for total beginners. A real teacher does a warm 2-minute intake so the person feels *welcomed, not sold to*.
4. **The ask comes only after it feels good** — a same-day message: *"Forfait Studio à 69,99 €/mois, 8 séances, sans engagement"*, plus free app access so non-converters stay in a re-nurture loop.
5. **Studio incentive** — partner studios are paid a fixed settlement (≈€8–12) per genuine trial attendee, so they welcome walk-ins and fill off-peak slots.
6. **Abuse cap** — one free trial per phone number (light OTP later).

## 5. Flexible Cancel / Pause — the "sans engagement" promise

Radical flexibility is the brand, not the fine print.

- **Cancel anytime** in 2 clicks — no retention call, no fee; access to period end (aligned with the "résiliation simplifiée" expectation).
- **Pause anytime** — freeze billing for travel, holidays, injury (up to 60 days/year).
- **Class roll-over** — up to 2 unused Studio classes carry to next month, then expire.
- **Switch tiers** up/down, prorated.
- **Per-class cancellation** — free up to 2h before; the class returns to your balance.
- **14-day withdrawal** (droit de rétractation) on new paid subscriptions.
- **No contract, no joining fee, no mid-term price hikes.**

## 6. Accessibility Levers (keeping it cheap, structurally)

- **Marketplace, not owned real estate** — revenue-share / per-attendance settlement with existing studios = zero rent, fills their off-peak empty slots.
- **Online-led cost base** — live + recorded classes are near-zero marginal cost, so €19 Online is profitable and subsidises discovery + the free trial.
- **Off-peak in-person pricing** — directs members to studios' dead hours (mid-morning, mid-afternoon): cheap for users, pure incremental revenue for studios.
- **Roll-over capped at 2** — boosts perceived value & retention without unbounded usage liability.
- **Bilingual FR/EN** — widens the addressable urban + expat market at no extra class cost.
- **Solidarity seats** — a small share of subsidised/free slots for students & low-income users, partly funded by Famille margins and optional "pay-it-forward" add-ons.

## 7. Unit-Economics Notes

Asset-light marketplace; the model lives on **blended margin per active member**, not per class.

- **In-person cost:** ≈€8–12/attended class settlement (off-peak fill). Studio (€69,99 for 8) implies ~€64–96 cost *if fully used* — so the plan is built on realistic **~50–65% utilisation** (typical class-pack behaviour), landing blended studio cost near **€40–60** and leaving positive gross margin after ~1.5–2.9% Stripe/SEPA fees. Roll-over (max 2, expiring) nudges retention while utilisation caps protect margin.
- **Online (live from India)** — teacher wages in India are a fraction of EU rates, so €60 for 8 live sessions (~€7.50 each) keeps a **high gross margin** while delivering authentic, real-time instruction; also a strong differentiator versus recorded-app competitors.
- **Free-trial CAC is bounded:** ~€8–12 studio settlement + €5–18 marketing per attendee ≈ **€13–30 per trier**. At a target **25–35% trial-to-paid**, blended **CAC per paying member ≈ €40–110**, with payback in **~1–2 months** at Studio/Famille margins.
- **Margin-protecting levers:** off-peak-only subsidised slots · utilisation caps (only Famille is unlimited, priced for heavy-use minority) · roll-over (not refunds) · annual plans for cash-flow & lower churn · free Découverte keeping non-payers in a near-zero-cost re-nurture loop.
- **Risks to watch:** trial no-shows (SMS confirmations) · Famille over-utilisation (cap classes/day) · studio supply density outside Paris (lead with Online where studios are sparse) · French VAT (TVA) treatment on memberships.

## 8. Competitive Landscape (France)

- **Premium studios / chains** (Tigre Yoga Club, Qee, Yuj, Le Tigre): €18–25/class, €100–160/month — we undercut sharply with no lock-in.
- **Aggregators** (ClassPass, Gymlib, Swile): credit/corporate access; we differentiate on owned membership, free trial, and price transparency, and can *also* list as a supply partner.
- **Apps** (Down Dog, OneFit-style): cheap but screen-only and impersonal — we win on real, in-person community.
- **Municipal / associatif classes**: cheap but inflexible schedules and waitlists — we win on flexibility, booking UX, and quality.

## 9. Growth

- **Waitlist reward stack** (surfaced at the form): (1) guaranteed FREE in-person trial, no card; (2) **Founding Member** — price locked for life; (3) priority on limited trial slots before public launch; (4) day-one app access; (5) **refer 3 friends → next month free** (viral loop).
- **Honest scarcity:** "Membres fondateurs · les premiers de votre ville" + city-by-city framing — urgency without fake countdowns.
- **City-by-city rollout:** signing up "fait venir le studio plus vite chez vous" — turns the waitlist into demand-aggregation per city (decide launch order by signup density).
- **Studio supply flywheel:** off-peak fill + per-attendance pay makes partnering low-risk → density → proximity → conversion.
- **Corporate channel (phase 2):** CSE/CE deals and Gymlib/Swile listing for B2B2C volume.

## 10. Compliance & Legal (France / EU) — before public launch

- **GDPR/RGPD:** consent collected explicitly at signup (checkbox, logged with `consent` + `locale` + timestamp). Add **Mentions légales**, **Politique de confidentialité**, and a cookie notice. Honour access/erasure requests; keep data minimal (name, phone, email, city).
- **Data residency:** host the Supabase project in an **EU region (e.g. Frankfurt `eu-central-1` or Paris)** so personal data stays in the EU.
- **Consumer law:** clear pre-contract info, 14-day withdrawal right, simplified online cancellation ("résiliation en 3 clics").
- **TVA:** confirm VAT treatment of memberships and the free trial with an accountant.
- **Sport/wellbeing:** ensure partner studios carry their own insurance and qualified teachers; a participation waiver/health note at first class.
